♠ Cambrio

How to play

Goal: have the lowest total when the round ends.

Card values

Red King (K♥︎ K♦︎) −1, Joker 0, Ace 1, 2–10 face value, Jack 11, Queen 12, Black King (K♠︎ K♣︎) 13.

Starting

Everyone gets 4 face-down cards (or 6, if the host picks that). You peek at your bottom row (2 cards, or 3 with 6 cards) for 10 seconds, or until you press I've got them. Then they go face down, so remember them!

Other players' hands are shown turned round, as if you sat across the table: their bottom row is the top row from your side.

Selecting cards

Tap a card to select it (it lights up gold). Tap another to move the selection. To select several cards at once, hold and drag across them. You need two cards for a J, Q or Black King power, and you can select several for a note.

On your turn

  • Press Draw (or tap the deck). Then either select one of your cards and press Swap to put the new card in its place (the old card goes on the discard pile), or press Discard to throw the new card away.
  • Or press Call Cambrio instead of drawing (tap twice to confirm). A big CAMBRIO! pops up for everyone and lands on your seat. Everyone else gets one last turn. Your cards can still be swapped, looked at and stuck.
  • You can't take cards from the discard pile.

Powers

A power works only when you draw the card from the deck and discard it straight away. Select the card(s), then press the power button.

  • 7 or 8: look at one of your own cards.
  • 9 or 10: look at one of another player's cards.
  • J or Q: swap any two cards without looking.
  • Black King: look at any two cards, then you may swap any two (or press Don't swap).

When anyone looks at a card it glows blue with 👁, and swapped cards glow orange with ⇄, for a few seconds. The log on the side says exactly which cards moved where.

Sticking

Select any card, yours or another player's, and press Stick if you think it matches the rank of the top discard. It flips over so everyone sees it. You can stick any time, as often as you like.

  • Right, on your own card: it leaves your hand.
  • Right, on another player's card: you then select one of your cards and press Give to hand it to them. Everything pauses until you do (a random card is given after 20 seconds).
  • Wrong: the card goes back where it was and you take a penalty card.

Timers

The host can set a turn timer (quick play uses 30 seconds). It restarts after every action on your turn. If it runs out, your turn is skipped and a drawn card is discarded without its power. Timers pause while someone owes a card.

End of the round

After the last turn there's one last chance to stick. It ends when the turn timer runs out, or sooner once everyone presses No more sticks (with no timer, only when everyone has pressed it). Then all cards are shown. The Cambrio caller wins ties. Any other tie goes to the player with the lowest single card.

Game options (private rooms)

The host can switch any of these on in the lobby:

  • Photographic memory: cards you have seen stay face up for you.
  • Card notes: select one or more cards, then press ✎ Note (or hold a card for half a second). Tap a rank once for isn't (red, crossed out) and twice for is (green); a third tap clears it. You can also mark who knows the card. Press Apply. With one card selected you edit that card's note; with several, the note is added to each. Only you see your notes, and they follow the card when it moves.
  • Card counter: a Cards left button under the log shows how many of each card haven't been discarded yet.
  • Score tracker: a line under your hand adds up the cards you can see or have noted as exactly one rank.

In every game

  • Emoji reactions: tap the 😀 button in the corner and pick an emoji. It floats up from your seat for everyone.
  • Stick reaction time: every correct stick shows how many seconds it took after the card landed on the pile.
  • Round replay: when the round ends, press ▶ Watch replay to step through every move again with all cards face up.
  • Bots (private rooms): the host can press + Add a bot in the lobby, and remove one with ✕. Bots only use cards they have really seen and the open discard pile. They count cards, and before big choices (keeping a card, calling Cambrio) they imagine many ways the round could go and pick what wins most often.

The host can also pick Play to 50 or 100 points. Rounds keep going and totals add up; once anyone reaches the target, the lowest total wins the match and the next game starts a new match from zero.

Coming back

If you lose connection or close the page, open the site again and press Rejoin on the main screen. You keep your seat and your cards. If you're away on your turn, it is skipped.

Tutorial and looks

The Tutorial button on the main screen walks through a turn step by step. Under Customise you can pick one of four card backs and four table colours. Only you see your choice, and it is saved on this device.

Quick play

Get matched with random players. A game starts straight away with 4 players, or with 2 or 3 once nobody new has joined for 10 seconds.
