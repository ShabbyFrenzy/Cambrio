♠ Cambrio

How to play

Everyone gets 4 face-down cards (or 6, if the host picks that). At the start you peek at your bottom row, then those cards go face down. Try to remember them!

Goal: have the lowest total when the round ends.

On your turn

  • Press Draw (or tap the deck), then select one of your cards and press Swap, or press Discard. Swapping puts the old card on the discard pile. You can't take from the discard pile.
  • Or call Cambrio instead of drawing. Everyone else gets one last turn. Your cards can still be swapped and stuck.

Powers

A power works only when you draw the card from the deck and discard it straight away.

  • 7 or 8: look at one of your own cards.
  • 9 or 10: look at one of another player's cards.
  • J or Q: swap any two cards without looking.
  • Black King: look at any two cards, then you may swap any two.

Sticking

Tap any card, yours or another player's, to select it (it lights up gold), then press Stick if you think it matches the rank of the top discard. It flips over so everyone sees it. Do it any time, as often as you like.

  • Stick your own card: it leaves your hand.
  • Stick another player's card: you then give them one of your cards.
  • Wrong card: it goes back where it was and you take a penalty card.

Ties

After the last turn, the sticking window lasts as long as the turn timer. With no timer, the cards are revealed once everyone presses No more sticks. The Cambrio caller wins ties. Any other tie goes to the player with the lowest single card.

Card values

Joker 0, Red King −1, Ace 1, 2–10 face value, Jack 11, Queen 12, Black King 13.
